Article in.
Video out.ពីអត្ថបទ ទៅជាវីដេអូ ក្នុងពាក្យបញ្ជាតែមួយ

STEP 1

Text

អត្ថបទ

Paste an article or a script. A news link works too: the text and photos come from the page.

STEP 2

Prepare

រៀបចំអត្ថបទ

Numbers become Khmer words, ៗ is expanded, and long sentences are split at natural pauses so nothing sounds rushed.

STEP 3

Voice

សំឡេង

Each line is spoken in the cloned voice you pick, with a greeting at the start and a sign-off at the end.

STEP 4

Video

វីដេអូ

A 1080×1920 video with captions in sync, animated cards, sound effects and loudness ready for TikTok.

$ ktts make texts/article.txt --voice rfi --images videos/images/article # → outputs/video_article/tiktok_rfi.mp4

Get startedចាប់ផ្តើមប្រើ

terminal
# install (Python 3.12 + ffmpeg)
$ python3.12 -m venv .venv-voxpocket
$ .venv-voxpocket/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
$ alias ktts='.venv-voxpocket/bin/python -m khmer_tts'

# speak, read, make a video
$ ktts voices
$ ktts speak --voice rfi "សួស្តីបងប្អូនទាំងអស់គ្នា។"
$ ktts read  --voice rfi texts/article.txt
$ ktts make  --voice rfi texts/article.txt

05

Questionsសំណួរដែលគេសួរញឹកញាប់

What is Khmer TTS?Khmer TTS ជាអ្វី?
A command-line tool that reads Khmer text aloud in a cloned voice, then turns the narration into a vertical video with captions, cards and sound effects.
How long a recording do I need to clone a voice?ត្រូវការសំឡេងប៉ុន្មានវិនាទី?
One speaker, 4 to 9 seconds of clean speech, and the exact words spoken in that clip. A wrong transcript is the main cause of a poor clone.
What hardware does it need?ត្រូវការកុំព្យូទ័របែបណា?
An NVIDIA GPU with 4 GB of memory or an Apple Silicon Mac, Python 3.12 and ffmpeg. The VoxPocket model (about 2 GB) downloads on first use and then runs on your own machine.
Can I clone anyone's voice?អាចចម្លងសំឡេងនរណាក៏បាន?
Only clone voices you have permission to use. Videos made with a cloned voice carry a "សំឡេង AI" tag; tick TikTok's AI-generated label when you post.
