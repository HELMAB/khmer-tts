Article in.
Video out.ពីអត្ថបទ ទៅជាវីដេអូ ក្នុងពាក្យបញ្ជាតែមួយ

STEP 1

Text

អត្ថបទ

Paste an article or a script. A news link works too: the text and photos come from the page.

STEP 2

Prepare

រៀបចំអត្ថបទ

Numbers become Khmer words, ៗ is expanded, and long sentences are split at natural pauses so nothing sounds rushed.

STEP 3

Voice

សំឡេង

Each line is spoken in the cloned voice you pick, with a greeting at the start and a sign-off at the end.

STEP 4

Video

វីដេអូ

A 1080×1920 video with captions in sync, animated cards, sound effects and loudness ready for TikTok.

$ ktts make texts/article.txt --voice rfi --images videos/images/article # → outputs/video_article/tiktok_rfi.mp4

04

Get startedចាប់ផ្តើមប្រើ

terminal
# install (Python 3.12 + ffmpeg)
$ python3.12 -m venv .venv-voxpocket
$ .venv-voxpocket/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
$ alias ktts='.venv-voxpocket/bin/python -m khmer_tts'

# speak, read, make a video
$ ktts voices
$ ktts speak --voice rfi "សួស្តីបងប្អូនទាំងអស់គ្នា។"
$ ktts read  --voice rfi texts/article.txt
$ ktts make  --voice rfi texts/article.txt
